Weather FAQ: Rain probability

The probability of precipitation is the likelihood of measurable
precipitation occurring at a specified location during a specified
forecast period. A 70% probability does not mean that it will rain
for 70% of the day.

Weather FAQ: Umbrella decisions

When deciding whether to carry an umbrella, consider the forecast
precipitation probability, expected rainfall amount, forecast period,
and the consequences of getting wet. A high probability of rain is
a reason to consider carrying an umbrella. A low probability does
not guarantee that it will stay dry.

Weather safety: Strong winds

Strong winds can make driving difficult, particularly for high-profile
vehicles, motorcycles, and vehicles crossing exposed bridges. Follow
official weather alerts and local road-safety guidance. Do not rely
on a general weather summary when an official warning is available.

Weather FAQ: Temperature

Maximum temperature is the highest temperature forecast for a period.
Minimum temperature is the lowest temperature forecast for that period.
These values describe the expected temperature range, not the exact
temperature at every hour.
